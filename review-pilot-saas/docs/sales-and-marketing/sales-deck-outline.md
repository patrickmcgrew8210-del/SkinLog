# Sales Deck Outline — ReviewPilot AI

10 slides, built for a live 1:1 or small-group demo call (pairs with
`demo-script.md`). Keep each slide to one idea — this deck supports the
conversation, it doesn't replace it.

1. **Title** — Logo, tagline ("Replies that sound like you. Approved in one
   tap."), your name/contact.
2. **The problem** — A real (anonymized or with permission) screenshot of a
   Google Business Profile with several unanswered reviews. One line:
   "Most owners know this is a problem. Nobody has 15 minutes a day for it."
3. **The cost of silence** — Bullet stats: owner responses increase
   conversion; review recency/engagement is a Google local-ranking signal;
   generic templated replies can read as worse than no reply.
4. **What ReviewPilot AI does** — Three-step visual (Connect → Teach your
   voice → Approve in one tap), matching the landing page's "how it works."
5. **Live draft example** — Screenshot of a real review next to its
   AI-drafted reply (use the prospect's own review live if demoing,
   otherwise a real anonymized example — never a fabricated-looking sample).
6. **You're always in control** — One slide, one message: negative reviews
   are never auto-posted, on any plan. This is the #1 objection-killer.
7. **Where it fits vs. alternatives** — Simple 3-column comparison: "Doing
   it yourself" (free, slow, inconsistent) / "Reputation suites like
   Podium/Birdeye" ($300-1000/mo, way more than needed) / "ReviewPilot AI"
   ($39-149/mo, does this one thing well).
8. **Pricing** — The three-tier table from the pricing page.
9. **What setup looks like** — "10 minutes: connect your Google listing,
   answer 4 questions about your tone, done."
10. **Call to action** — "Start your 14-day free trial today" with a QR
    code or short link to `/pricing`, plus your direct contact info for
    questions.

## Format notes

- Build this in Google Slides or Keynote — no need for design software.
  Reuse the brand palette (`brand-500` #2f6fed for accents, slate for text)
  and the logo/icon files in `review-pilot-saas/brand/`.
- Keep total deck under 10 slides for a live call; anything longer competes
  with the conversation instead of supporting it.
