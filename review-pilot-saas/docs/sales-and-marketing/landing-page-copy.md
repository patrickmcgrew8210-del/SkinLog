>

## Solution (how it works)

**Section headline:** Three steps. Ten minutes. Then it just runs.

1. **Connect your Google Business Profile.** One click, secure OAuth login —
   we never see your Google password.
2. **Teach it your voice.** Paste a few replies you've written before, or
   answer four quick questions about your tone. That's it.
3. **Approve from your phone.** New review comes in, a draft is ready
   within minutes. Tap approve, edit-then-approve, or skip. Nothing posts
   without you.
