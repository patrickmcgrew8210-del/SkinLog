## Full description

Track your skin the way a dermatologist actually wants to see it.

SkinLog is a private, personal journal for your skin — log breakouts,
redness, and routine adherence in seconds, then watch patterns emerge over
weeks and months instead of guessing from memory.

WHAT YOU CAN TRACK
• Daily breakout severity and redness/irritation, rated simply
• Exactly where breakouts show up, on a tappable face map
• Your AM/PM routine — what you actually did, not just planned to
• Products you're using, so you can connect a new product to a change in
  your skin
• Diet notes, stress triggers, sleep, and (optionally) cycle day
• Before/after photos, compared side by side

SEE THE PATTERNS
Trends turns weeks of entries into an actual picture: severity over time,
a calendar heatmap of your worst and best days, and plain-language
correlations — "breakouts tend to follow poor sleep," not a spreadsheet
you have to interpret yourself.

BRING IT TO YOUR DERMATOLOGIST
One tap generates a clean, printable report summarizing your history —
routine adherence, trigger notes, affected areas, and photos — so your
next appointment starts from real data instead of "it's been bad
lately."

YOUR DATA, YOUR CONTROL
Your account syncs securely across your devices. Export everything as a
backup file whenever you want, and delete your account and all your data
permanently, right from the app, any time.

No ads. No tracking. No selling your data. It's your skin and your
information — SkinLog just helps you see it clearly.

## App Privacy (Apple) / Data Safety (Google Play) form — how to answer

Both stores ask a structured questionnaire about what data you collect.
Answer based on what's actually true for this build:

**Data collected:**
- Contact info: Email address, Name — used for account creation, not
  linked to advertising, not used for tracking
- Health & fitness: skin condition entries, photos — used for the app's
  core functionality only, not linked to advertising, not used for
  tracking
- User content: photos, free-text notes — same as above

**Data NOT collected:** location, browsing history, contacts, financial
info, advertising identifiers, analytics/usage data, crash data (unless
you later add a crash reporter — don't check this box unless you do)

**Third parties data is shared with:** Supabase (your backend
infrastructure provider — this is a processor, not a third party you're
"sharing" data with in the advertising sense, but disclose it as your
hosting/database provider if the form asks)

**Data linked to identity:** Yes — behind your account (email/password
or Google sign-in)

**Data used to track you across apps/websites:** No

**Account deletion:** Yes, available in-app (this satisfies both
platforms' requirement directly — Apple's guideline 5.1.1(v) and
Google Play's account deletion policy)
