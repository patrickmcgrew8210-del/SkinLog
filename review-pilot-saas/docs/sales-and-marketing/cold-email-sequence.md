# Cold Email Sequence — ReviewPilot AI

Target: owners/managers of dentists, HVAC/plumbing, roofing, med spas,
restaurants with ≥ 15 Google reviews (enough review volume that unanswered
replies are visibly a problem; find leads via Google Maps search + a
scraper/manual list, or a lead tool like Apollo filtered by industry + city).

Send from a real, warmed-up email address (not a fresh domain — inbox
placement matters more than volume here). 3-email sequence, 3 business days
apart. Stop the moment someone replies, positive or negative.

---

## Email 1 — Direct, specific, short

**Subject:** noticed a few unanswered reviews for [Business Name]

Hi [First Name],

I was looking at [Business Name]'s Google reviews and noticed a handful
from the last month or two that haven't gotten a reply yet — including a
couple of really nice ones from [specific detail if visible, e.g. "a
patient who mentioned your front desk by name"].

I built a small tool that drafts replies in your own voice and lets you
approve them from your phone in a few seconds — no typing required unless
you want to change something. Most owners clear a backlog of 10-20 reviews
in about 5 minutes the first time they use it.

Worth a quick look? Happy to show you on your actual reviews, not a demo
account: [demo link]

[Your name]

write them.

## Notes on execution

- Personalize the bracketed details for real — a sequence that clearly
  wasn't customized gets ignored or reported as spam. Pulling the business
  name and one real review detail from Google Maps takes under a minute per
  lead.
- Send volume: start at 20-30/day from a single mailbox to protect
  deliverability; scale by adding mailboxes, not by increasing per-mailbox
  volume.
- Track replies in a simple spreadsheet (or a free-tier CRM) with columns:
  business, contact, sent dates, reply, outcome.
- A/B test subject lines after the first 100 sends; keep whichever gets a
  higher open rate for the next batch.
