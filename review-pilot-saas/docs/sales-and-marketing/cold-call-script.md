# Cold Call Script — ReviewPilot AI

Best used as a follow-up to a cold email that got no reply, or as a
cold-cold call to a business found via Google Maps search. Keep it
conversational — this is a script to internalize, not read verbatim.

## Opening (get permission to continue)

> "Hi, is this [Owner/Manager Name]? Hey, this is [Your Name] — I know I'm
> calling out of nowhere, do you have 30 seconds, or is this a bad time?"

*(If bad time: "No problem — is there a better time today or tomorrow I
could grab two minutes?" Get a specific time, hang up, call back exactly
then.)*

## The hook (10-15 seconds)

> "Quick reason for the call — I was looking at [Business Name]'s Google
> reviews and noticed a few from the last couple months that never got a
> reply. I build a tool that writes review replies in your own voice and
> lets you approve them from your phone in a couple seconds. Is that
> something you handle yourself right now, or does someone on your team do
> it?"

*(This question does two things: qualifies whether they even think about
this, and gets them talking.)*

## Common responses and how to handle them

**"We don't really have time for that."**
> "That's exactly the problem it solves — it's not asking you to write
> anything, just approve a draft that's already written. Most people spend
> under a minute a day on it."

**"We just use a generic template / do it ourselves."**
> "Makes sense — a lot of people do. The difference is customers can
> usually tell when a reply is templated, and it can read as insincere.
> This one's trained on how you actually write, so it doesn't sound
> canned."

**"How much does it cost?"**
> "Plans start at $39 a month, and there's a 14-day free trial so you can
> see actual drafts on your actual reviews before paying anything. No
> contract — cancel anytime."

**"Not interested."**
> "Totally fair — mind if I ask, is it because you've already got this
> handled, or just not a priority right now?" *(Learn something, then
> exit gracefully: "Appreciate your time — have a good one.")*

**"Send me some info."**
> "Happy to — what's the best email? I'll send over a link where you can
> actually connect your Google listing and see real drafts, takes about 10
> minutes." *(Get the email, send the demo link immediately while still on
> the call if possible.)*

## Close (always ask for a specific next step)

> "Would it make sense to grab 15 minutes this week where I show you drafts
> on your actual reviews — not a demo account, your real ones? I've got
> [day/time] or [day/time] open."

## Rules for the call itself

- Never pitch past 60-90 seconds without letting them talk.
- Always end with a specific next action (a booked time, a sent email,
  or an honest "not now") — never a vague "I'll follow up."
- Log every call outcome immediately (business, outcome, next step, date)
  so nothing falls through.
