# Live Demo Script — ReviewPilot AI

Goal: in 10-15 minutes, show a draft reply generated on the prospect's own
real reviews, and get either a trial start or a specific next step. Never
demo on fake/sample data if you can help it — pulling their actual Google
reviews is the single biggest credibility move available.

## Before the call

- Pull up the prospect's Google Business Profile / Maps listing.
- Identify 2-3 real reviews: one glowing 5-star, one flat/generic 3-star,
  and if one exists, a 1-2 star review (this is the moment that sells the
  "human always approves negative reviews" feature).
- Have the dashboard (or, pre-launch, a working local build) open and ready.

## 1. Open with their problem, not your product (2 min)

> "Before I show you anything — how are you currently handling review
> replies today? ... Yeah, that's what I hear from almost everyone I talk
> to. Let me show you what this actually looks like on your reviews,
> specifically."

## 2. Show the 5-star draft (3 min)

- Read the real review out loud.
- Show the AI-generated draft next to it.
- Point out: it references specifics from the review, it's not generic,
  and it's in a tone that could plausibly be the owner's own voice.

> "This didn't come from a template — it read the actual review and wrote
> something specific to it. If you don't like a word choice, you tap edit,
> fix it, and it remembers that for next time."

## 3. Show the 1-2 star review, if one exists (3 min) — this is the trust moment

> "Now here's the one that matters most. Negative reviews — this never
> auto-posts, on any plan, ever. It drafts something for you, but a human
> always has to read it and hit approve. You're never going to wake up to
> find out the AI said something publicly on your behalf that you didn't
> see first."

*(This single point resolves the #1 objection before it's even raised —
say it clearly and let it land.)*

## 4. One-click approve (2 min)

- Show approving a draft and (if integration is live) posting it, or the
  copy-to-clipboard flow for platforms without direct posting (Yelp).

> "That's it — that's the whole workflow. Most people spend under a minute
> a day here once the backlog's cleared."

## 5. Pricing and close (3 min)

> "Plans start at $39 a month, most businesses your size land on Growth at
> $79. There's a 14-day free trial, no contract, cancel anytime. Want me to
> get you set up right now, or do you want to try it yourself first?"

- If yes: walk them through signup live, on the call.
- If hesitant: "No pressure — want me to send the trial link and follow up
  Thursday?" Get a specific day, and actually follow up that day.

## Objection handling cheat sheet

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "We already use [Podium/Birdeye]." | "How much are you paying for that? Most people are paying $300+ for a suite where review replies are one small feature. This is $39-149 and it's the one thing we do, really well." |
| "What if it says something wrong?" | "You approve every reply before it posts — and negative reviews always require approval, no exceptions, on any plan." |
| "I don't trust AI with my brand voice." | "That's exactly why setup starts with feeding it your actual past replies — it's not writing in a generic voice, it's writing in yours. And you can always edit before approving." |
| "Can I think about it?" | "Of course — want me to start your 14-day trial now so the clock doesn't start until you're ready to look at it, or would a follow-up call work better?" |
